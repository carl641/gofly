About MSL

Welcome to the Shoals!

When you are traveling through Northwest Alabama Regional Airport (MSL), take advantage of our free Wi-Fi, flat screen TVs, convenient free parking, comfortable passenger seating, on-site rental cars, clean terminals, great local dining and shopping, and true southern hospitality.

  • Free Wi-Fi
  • Free parking
  • On-site rental cars
  • Flat screen TVs
  • Comfortable seating
  • Southern hospitality

Here at MSL, we take great pride in our role as the gateway to the Shoals Area. Our convenient location is just minutes from downtown’s many regional businesses and local entertainment venues. Recognized nationally for the Tennessee Valley Authority and the “Muscle Shoals Sound” music heritage, the Shoals Area is a family-friendly place for residents and visitors to live, work, and play.

Whether you’re traveling through MSL for business or pleasure, we will make sure you spend less time traveling and more time enjoying your stay. Conveniently located by US Hwy 20 (Alt Hwy 72), the airport offers quick and easy access to surrounding communities.

Whether you’re flying private or commercial, our friendly airport staff will make you feel at home in the Shoals.
